About Cozy Small Home

Cozy Small Home is a small-space home decor blog for people who live in small apartments and tiny rooms — and still want them to feel like home.

We write for renters in the United States, especially women aged 25 to 44 who are decorating a first apartment, a studio, a small bedroom or a shared home on a real budget. Most of the ideas here cost between $25 and $200, and most of them are renter-friendly: no drilling into the landlord's walls, no permanent changes, nothing that risks your deposit.

What we cover

  • Small bedroom ideas that actually work in rooms under 120 square feet
  • Small kitchen and pantry organization
  • Small bathroom storage
  • Small living room storage and layout
  • Budget makeovers and before-and-after projects

How we choose products

We look for things that are affordable, easy to find in the US, and practical in a small space — storage that folds away, furniture that does two jobs, and organizers that fit real closets and cabinets, not showroom ones. Prices change, so always check the current price before you buy.

How this site makes money

Cozy Small Home earns money in two ways: display ads, and affiliate commissions when a reader buys a product through a link on this site. That does not change what we recommend, and it never costs you extra. Read our Disclaimer and Privacy Policy for the full details.
